Our work

Who your gift is for

We want to stand with people who cannot meet their basic needs on their own.

A meal for a hungry family

When there is no work, many daily-wage families go hungry, and floods or disasters make it worse. Your gift helps ease that hardship.

Treatment for the sick

Medicine and treatment are out of reach for many. Your gift can help a patient get care on time.

A future for orphans and poor children

Books, uniforms or fee support can keep an orphan child in school. Your gift keeps the path to learning open.
